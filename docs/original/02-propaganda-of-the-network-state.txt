## Genesis Mint

Today we announce the details of our genesis mint, the establishment of our studio, a DAO designed to govern the token DRAKMA, commercial NFT rights, as well as the CITADEL RELIK. But first, we are pleased to announce the details of our mint.
- The CITADEL genesis mint will be 1024 1/1 CITADEL NFT. Each CITADEL will be a completely unique and original piece of art designed by co-founder and artist 5TYX.

- The CITADEL genesis mint will be free to the public.

CITADEL is a post-human metaverse launching on Ethereum in early 2022. We believe making the genesis mint free to the public is the best way to stay authentic to web3, build trust with our growing community, and give ourselves the flexibility to experiment. 
Limiting our mint size to 1024 1/1 CITADEL puts the focus on the unique, enigmatic, and post-human artwork of co-founder, 5TYX. 

## THE NETWORK STATE

We are pleased to announce the formation of our studio, THE NETWORK STATE. The studio is co-owned by the three founders of CITADEL and remains completely self-funded. As we forgo primary revenue from our genesis mint by making CITADEL free to the public, we are required to invent alternative models to allow us to keep experimenting with the world of CITADEL. Today, we announce the CITADEL RELIK.

## CITADEL RELIK

The CITADEL RELIK consist of 64 ancient and mysterious CITADEL. Each RELIK belongs to the CITADEL ELEKT, the aristocracy of ancient CITADEL. The RELIK depict a 1/1 cinematic scene from this archaic world. Each RELIK owner will be airdropped one of the 64 CITADEL making up the ELEKT on the day of the CITADEL NFT mint.
Each RELIK is a key-holder to an ancient technology tree and will be equipped with an owner-only property to unlock it. This key will be written in sanskrit on-chain.
In-game, the RELIK will act as a research center progressing the discovery of an ancient technology tree. This technology tree will be available to all members of the FACTION and will unlock different aspects of the citadel, fleet, intelligence network, and diplomacy. 
RELIK technology can be stacked, creating an asymmetric technology advantage in-game. Some technology will be more ancient and rare than others. These strategic properties make the RELIK both the most protected and most sieged CITADEL in-game. 
Monarchs of the ELEKT, the RELIK NFT will also have unique and multiplicative voting rights in the DAO, GRID MAELSTROM.

## GRID MAELSTROM

GRID MAELSTROM is a blockchain SECRET SOCIETY. This society will govern the token DRAKMA in the form of a DAO. GRID MAELSTROM’s primary purpose is funding the game and other ratified projects through issuance of the in-game currency DRAKMA. 
Future PROPOGANDA will cover final details of this enigmatic decentralized organization.

## Commercial Rights

The owner of each CITADEL NFT has full commercial rights over their NFT. The owner of each CITADEL RELIK has full commercial rights over the cinematic scene depicted in the NFT.
