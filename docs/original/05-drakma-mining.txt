## Mining DRAKMA

Lite your CITADEL to the grid by using citadel os. You must make two choices when staking your CITADEL. The first choice is your faction. There are currently four factions in the world of CITADEL.
annexation
autonomous zone
network state
sanction
The second choice is the technology your staked CITADEL will research. Each CITADEL is equipped with a tech property. Staking your CITADEL to the technology your CITADEL possesses will give each CITADEL you stake a 2X research multiple. The eight technology branches in CITADEL are as follows.
preservative algorithms
militant algorithms
antimatter annihilation
propulsion
posthumanism
ecological extraction
technocracy
proginator psi
Staking a CITADEL is a two step process. When you stake your CITADEL, it is transferred to the EXORDIUM contract. You must first approve your CITADEL to be transferred. Follow these examples to start mining DRAKMA, an ERC20 token, through our EXORDIUM contract. Each command triggers an Ethereum transaction. You must wait for each transaction to be confirmed before starting the next. 

#### Stake 1 CITADEL (#40) to the network state, posthumanism

citadel os> approve 40
citadel os> stake network state, posthumanism, 40

#### Stake 1 CITADEL (#750) to the sanction, militant algorithms

citadel os> approve 750
citadel os> stake sanction, militant algorithms, 750
Wallets with many CITADEL can stake them all at once.

#### Stake 4 CITADEL (#0, #10, #56, #88) to the autonomous zone, propulsion

citadel os> approve 0
citadel os> approve 10
citadel os> approve 56
citadel os> approve 88
citadel os> stake autonomous zone, propulsion, 0 10 56 88

#### Stake 2 CITADEL (#800, #801) to the annexation, proginator psi

citadel os> approve 800
citadel os> approve 801
citadel os> stake annexation, proginator psi, 800 801

## Claiming DRAKMA

To claim your DRAKMA, you must already have CITADEL staked to the EXORDIUM contract. You are free to claim DRAKMA from the contract as frequently as you like. The claim command in citadel os will transfer the DRAKMA you have earned to your wallet, and register the research your CITADEL have conducted for your faction. Claiming DRAKMA leaves your CITADEL staked to the contract.
citadel os> claim

## Withdrawing CITADEL

You can withdraw your CITADEL at any point during the EXORDIUM. Withdrawing your CITADEL transfers them from the EXORDIUM contract back to your wallet. 

#### Withdraw 1 CITADEL (#750)

citadel os> withdraw 750

#### Withdraw 4 CITADEL (#0, #10, #56, #88)

citadel os> withdraw 0 10 56 88

## Tips

The EXORDIUM contract is optimized for low gas transactions. There are a few tips to follow to maximize research gained when claiming. 

#### Staking and Withdrawing

Staking and withdrawing CITADEL resets your techTreeMultiple. Therefor, it is advised to stake and withdraw all of your CITADEL at once. Meaning, if you have 4 CITADEL, it is advised to stake them all at once, and withdraw them all at once. You are able to add CITADEL to your staking pool, or withdraw individual CITADEL from your staking pool, but doing so will reset your tech multiplier. It will not affect the DRAKMA you gain.

#### Claiming

Claim as frequently as you like, but if you plan to withdraw, claim just before you withdraw. This will preserve your tech multiplier, if you have one.

#### Approving

Token approval is removed from the NFT when you stake. You need to approve your NFT for staking EACH time you stake. So if you approve, stake, and withdraw, you will need to approve again before you stake for a second time.
