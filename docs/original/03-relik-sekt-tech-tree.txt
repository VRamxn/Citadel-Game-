## RELIK

The CITADEL RELIK hold the key (पक्ष) to unlock the CITADEL tech tree. Factions will recruit RELIK to accelerate discovery of ancient technology, giving the faction an asymmetric in-game advantage. The thirst to obtain certain rare keys (पुरातन) will create undeniable tension and conflict. If a faction cannot peacefully recruit a RELIK, they will choose violence to obtain it. This will make the RELIK both the most assaulted and protected CITADEL in-game.

## RELIK AUCTION

The first 64 CITADEL minted will be RELIK. These 1/1 CITADEL will be minted to the game treasury wallet and be auctioned to the public immediately after the CITADEL mint. The RELIK auction will be used to fund artists and developers to accelerate game & metaverse development. 
The winner of each auction will be airdropped a cinematic scene from the ancient world of CITADEL immediately after the auction. The owner of each NFT maintains commercial rights. It is our current intention to give RELIK cinematic scene owners proposal initiation rights to the CITADEL DAO, GRID MAELSTROM.

## SEKT

Each CITADEL belongs to a SEKT. The CITADEL RELIK is the aristocracy of this world consisting of 64 CITADEL. These ancient and mysterious CITADEL form the totality of this enigmatic elite. The DROTH XYL, IONSLAUGHT, and ELEKT follow in rarity. 
Each SEKT has a multiplier used to distribute the in-game currency DRAKMA during the prelude to the game. CITADEL is a strategy game of conquest where wallets will be constantly upgrading and expanding. DRAKMA will be used for all upgrades. The more DRAKMA a wallet possesses, the faster they will be able to gain control of the GRID.

## CITADEL TECH TREE

The CITADEL RELIK hold the key (पक्ष) to each layer 0 technology. 64 gen0 RELIK exist in the CITADEL ecosystem. Factions lacking a RELIK in a key technology category like militant algorithms, will not be able to advance the tech tree of this key category. These rules will make RELIK both the most protected and most assaulted CITADEL in-game.

#### Tech Tree Rules

- The CITADEL tech tree is designed to unlock asymmetric technology advancements for an entire faction.

- At least one RELIK unlocks every layer 0 technology. Some RELIK are more rare than others.

- Each technology can be researched from level 0 to level 9. This research is done collaboratively with the entire faction & RELIK.

- When level9 is reached, the next layers of the technology are unlocked.

- Each researched technology unlocks access to new weapons, fleet, and game accelerants.

- What lies beneath a technology may be more powerful than the technology that unlocked it.

There are 7 technology trees in CITADEL. Research in the layer 0 technology of the tree will unlock layer 1. Additional trees and layers will be introduced with each generation of CITADEL.
